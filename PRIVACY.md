# Privacy Policy — Vivum

**Last updated: October 30, 2026**

Vivum is a Bible reading app (Tauri + Angular) that works
fully **offline**. We do not collect, transmit, or sell
any personal data.

## 1. Data we collect

None. Vivum has no accounts and asks for no name, email, or
personal information. No usage data is ever sent to a server —
we operate none.

## 2. Offline operation

The entire biblical text is bundled inside the app. Reading,
searching, and browsing work without an Internet connection and
generate no network traffic.

## 3. Data stored on your device

Your preferences stay exclusively on your device, in the app's
local storage (`localStorage`):

- Favorite verses (reference, version, text, and date added);
- Chosen display language, last reading position and a short
  recently-read history (used by the "Continue reading" shortcut);
- These settings and the favorites list can be exported by you at
  any time from the Favorites page. The export is written locally
  and is never uploaded anywhere.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.
It is deleted if you use "Delete my data" in the app, clear the
app data, or uninstall the app.

## 4. Tracking, cookies, and advertising

Vivum includes no trackers, no third-party cookies, no analytics
tools, and no advertising. No advertising identifiers or audience
measurement are used.

## 5. External links

The app contains links to GitHub (project page
`https://github.com/Martzcode/Vivum` and developer profile
`https://github.com/Martzcode`). If you click these links, your
browser is subject to GitHub's privacy policy, which is
independent of this policy.

## 6. Children

Vivum is suitable for all ages and knowingly collects no data
about children.

## 7. Your rights (GDPR)

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is
nothing for us to review, export, or correct. You stay in full
control: you can delete your favorites and preferences at any
time from within the app or by clearing local data.

## 8. Contact and changes

For any question about this policy, open an issue at
https://github.com/Martzcode/Vivum.

If this policy changes, the update date above will be revised.
The text shown in the app (`/privacy` route, available from the
About page) prevails and is translated into French, English,
Spanish, German, and Malagasy.
